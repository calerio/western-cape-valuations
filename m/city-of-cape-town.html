City of Cape Town property valuations

Unlike the Western Cape's local municipalities, the City of Cape Town does not publish a downloadable valuation roll — so the metro has no aggregate dashboard in this atlas. Its official per-property search is free to use.

How to look up a Cape Town valuation

The City publishes its general valuation roll for inspection one property at a time through its own online search — there is no downloadable roll, so Cape Town's 24 sibling municipalities appear in this atlas while the metro does not (yet).

Search the City's valuation roll ↗ Satellite map

Values in the City's roll are municipal valuations as at its valuation date — set for rates, not today's sale prices.

Bulk data: in progress

A PAIA (Promotion of Access to Information Act) request for the City's full GV2022 and GV2025 valuation rolls is in progress (reference 100087816). If the data is released, Cape Town will join the atlas with the same searchable dashboards as every other municipality. Last checked: 2026-07-13.

How these valuations work

Municipal valuations estimate each property’s open-market value on a set past date (mass appraisal under the Municipal Property Rates Act) — calculated to charge rates, not a floor or a live sale price. How valuations work →
