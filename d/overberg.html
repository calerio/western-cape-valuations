How these valuations work

Municipal valuations estimate each property’s open-market value on a set past date (mass appraisal under the Municipal Property Rates Act) — calculated to charge rates, not a floor or a live sale price. How valuations work →

Who governs these values

Other role-players: Western Cape Valuation Appeal Board ↗ · Western Cape Department of Local Government ↗ · CoGTA (Cooperative Governance & Traditional Affairs) ↗. Appeals against the valuer’s decision go to the Valuation Appeal Board.
